Rule 4 / 7

Consistency builds recognition.

  • Same colors everywhere
  • Same voice in every caption
  • Same feeling — even when the product changes
  • Same grid, same rhythm, same rules
  • Same logo placement, same proportions

The interrupted line is the one people remember. Break the pattern once — on purpose.
