# WorqAI — Brand Context

## What WorqAI Is

WorqAI is a resume builder SaaS that uses AI to help job seekers in LATAM and US Hispanic markets create ATS-optimized resumes and LinkedIn profiles that actually get interviews. Unlike generic US-centric tools, WorqAI understands LATAM formatting conventions, bilingual CVs, and the specific challenges of applying to remote roles or US companies from Latin America.

## The Two Lanes

Kenneth runs two related businesses — keep these separate in marketing:

- **WorqAI (primary)** — SaaS product. Self-serve. Subscription. Target: anyone in LATAM who wants a better resume fast. Marketing goal: sign-ups, activations, paid conversions.
- **Profile Pro LATAM (secondary service)** — Done-for-you service. High-touch. Per-project pricing. Target: mid-senior professionals who'd rather pay an expert than DIY. Marketing goal: qualified leads via Reddit, Instagram, Facebook.

Both share audience overlap but have different offers, pricing, and funnels. WorqAI ads point to sign-up. Profile Pro ads point to a consultation/audit.

## ICP — WorqAI

**Primary:** LATAM professionals 25-40, Spanish-first, applying to remote roles or LATAM corporates. Tech-savvy enough to use a SaaS but tired of paying $30/mo for Rezi or Resume.io when they want bilingual + LATAM formatting.

**Secondary:** US Hispanic job seekers who want tools in Spanish with bilingual output.

**Pain points they have:**
- English resume builders that don't understand LATAM work history conventions
- ATS systems filtering their CVs because formatting is off
- No guidance on LinkedIn optimization in Spanish
- Remote job applications requiring polished English CVs they can't produce
- Generic AI tools (ChatGPT) that produce AI-slop resumes recruiters reject

**What they want:**
- Fast (resume in under 10 minutes)
- Bilingual (toggle ES/EN)
- ATS-safe (templates that parse correctly)
- LATAM-aware (month/year dates, no photo toggleable, degree naming conventions)
- Affordable ($10-15/mo or one-time)

## Positioning

**Category:** AI resume builder
**Differentiation:** Only resume builder built for LATAM + bilingual + remote-first job search
**Promise:** A recruiter-ready, ATS-safe resume in Spanish or English in under 10 minutes

**The one-liner:** "El resume builder hecho para latinos que buscan trabajo remoto."

## Voice (English)

Direct, warm, slightly irreverent. Talk like a helpful friend who happens to know recruiting. Never corporate. Never hype. Short sentences. Concrete examples over abstractions. Humor when it fits, never forced.

## Voz (Spanish)

Cercano, directo, sin formalidad exagerada. Tuteo por defecto (no "usted"). Evitá el español neutro telenovelesco — usá un tono latinoamericano natural, con modismos leves cuando ayudan ("listo", "ya", "dale"). Nunca "liberá tu potencial" ni "transformá tu carrera".

## Competitors

- **Rezi, Teal, Enhancv, Resume.io** — English-only, US-centric, no LATAM formatting awareness
- **Kickresume** — Multi-language but weak Spanish + no LATAM context
- **CV Maker** — Free but dated UI, no AI
- **ChatGPT + copy/paste** — The real competitor. Free but slow and slop-prone.

**Our wedge:** Bilingual output + LATAM conventions + speed + ATS safety. None of the competitors nail all four.

## Pricing (working draft)

- **Free** — 1 resume download/month, watermarked, ES only
- **Pro ($9/mo or $79/yr)** — Unlimited downloads, both languages, ATS templates, LinkedIn optimizer
- **One-time ($19)** — Single polished resume + LinkedIn audit, no subscription
- **Service upsell** — Done-for-you by Profile Pro LATAM ($149-399)

## Never Do

- Generic SaaS copy ("streamline your job search", "unlock your career potential")
- Stock photos of diverse professionals at laptops
- Any variant of "revolutionize", "transform", "empower"
- English-only content without a Spanish version for LATAM audiences
- Ad claims we can't back up ("get hired 10x faster")
