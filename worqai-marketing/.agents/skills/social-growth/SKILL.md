## Step-by-Step Workflow

### Step 1 — Confirm niche positioning before any content

State this positioning on every account. Do not deviate:
- Niche: "Conseguir trabajo más rápido usando IA y CVs optimizados."
- Angle: Technical explainer, not motivational coach. Show HOW the ATS system works.
- Differentiation: Real scores, real before/afters, real client results. No stock footage.
- Voice: Direct, slightly informal, one specific human per post — not a crowd.

If the user's content idea drifts off-niche, flag it and redirect before writing anything.

### Step 2 — Assign the content to one of 4 pillars

Every piece of content belongs to exactly one pillar. No exceptions.

| Pillar | Goal | Formats |
|---|---|---|
| Educación ATS | Build authority, explain the system | Carruseles, Reels educativos |
| Antes / Después | Build trust with proof | Carruseles, capturas de pantalla |
| Mercado laboral | Drive shares with hot takes | Reels cortos, texto |
| Oferta directa | Convert to DM or app signup | Stories CTA, posts de oferta |

Ratio: 40% educación / 30% antes-después / 20% mercado / 10% oferta.

### Step 3 — Write the hook first, everything else second

The hook is the first 1–3 seconds of a Reel/TikTok or the first slide of a carousel.
No hook = no reach. Write it before the body every time.

Use one of these 5 hook types:

1. **Result:** "Mi cliente llevaba 4 meses sin entrevistas. Cambiamos su CV y en 6 días tenía 2 llamadas."
2. **Loss/Fear:** "El 75% de CVs son rechazados antes de que un humano los vea. El tuyo puede ser uno."
3. **Question:** "¿Sabés cuánto tiempo dedica un reclutador a leer tu CV? 7 segundos."
4. **Contrarian:** "Mandar 100 CVs es una pérdida de tiempo si tenés este error de formato."
5. **Curiosity:** "Hay una sola línea en tu CV que decide si pasás o no el filtro automático..."

Hook formula: [Número o dato] + [Dolor específico] + [Promesa implícita]

### Step 4 — Build the content piece using the correct format spec

**Reel / TikTok (15–45 segundos):**
- 0–3 sec: Hook. No intro. No "hola soy". Dato o resultado directo.
- 3–20 sec: El problema + una solución concreta. Máximo 2 puntos.
- 20–35 sec: Prueba — screenshot, score, antes/después, número real.
- 35–45 sec: CTA único. "Comentá SCORE y te mando el análisis gratis."

**Carousel Instagram (8 slides):**
- Slide 1: Hook — una frase bold en fondo oscuro. Nada más.
- Slide 2: Dato duro. Una estadística con fuente o contexto.
- Slides 3–6: Un error por slide + una línea de solución. Formato ❌ / ✅.
- Slide 7: Prueba — resultado de cliente anonimizado con números.
- Slide 8: CTA único — "Escribime AUDITORÍA por DM" o "Escaneá tu CV gratis [link en bio]."

**Stories (diario):**
- Poll: "¿Cuánto tiempo llevás buscando trabajo? / Menos de 1 mes / Más de 1 mes"
- Follow-up story: CTA directo al DM con oferta de auditoría o app scan.
- Urgency frame: "5 cupos esta semana — primeros 5 que escriban."

### Step 5 — Add hashtags and SEO caption

**Instagram (3–5 hashtags, never more):**
- 1–2 broad: #jobsearch #empleo #trabajoremoto #career
- 1–2 niche: #resumetips #cvoptimizado #careercoach #ats #buscandotrabajo
- 1 branded: #CVAppLatam or #ProfileProLATAM

**TikTok (3–5 hashtags):**
- Core: #careertok #resumetips #jobsearch
- Local: #trabajocostarica #empleolatam #trabajoremoto
- Branded: #CVAppLatam

**SEO caption rule:** Write the exact phrase the target would search.
Good: "Cómo pasar el ATS con tu CV en Costa Rica 2026"
Bad: "tips de trabajo #empleo"

Instagram and TikTok both index caption text. The caption IS the SEO.

### Step 6 — Assign posting slot using weekly schedule

**Instagram (5–7 posts/week):**
- Mon / Wed / Fri: Reel (discovery, top of funnel)
- Tue / Thu: Carousel (saves, mid funnel)
- Daily: 1–3 Stories (DM CTAs, polls, behind the scenes)

**TikTok (4–6 posts/week):**
- Every Reel produced gets cross-posted to TikTok 30 min later
- Add 1 TikTok-native video/week using trending audio adapted to the niche

**Facebook (3 posts/week + daily group activity):**
- Recycle top Instagram post of the week × 3
- Daily: Answer questions in Costa Rica employment groups. Add value first. Offer DM audit second.

### Step 7 — Add the CTA and DM funnel trigger

Every post must have exactly ONE CTA. Not two. Not zero.

Rotate these CTAs across posts:
- "Comentá SCORE" → ManyChat auto-DM delivers free ATS scan info + qualifies lead
- "Comentá CV" → ManyChat sends 3-error audit offer
- "Comentá APP" → ManyChat sends early access link to the app
- "Escribime AUDITORÍA" → Manual 1:1 audit (Profile Pro service)
- "Link en bio" → App free scan landing page

**ManyChat flow structure:**
1. User comments keyword on post
2. Bot auto-DMs: "¡Hola! Vi que querés revisar tu CV. ¿Te interesa el análisis ATS gratis o el acceso al app?"
3. Bot collects: CV or LinkedIn URL
4. Tag contact as "lead-caliente" in CRM
5. Human follow-up within 24 hours with paid offer

### Step 8 — Run collaboration sprint monthly

Do this every 30 days to spike followers outside your existing audience.

1. Build list of 50–100 accounts: same niche, 5k–50k followers, real engagement (check comments, not just likes)
2. Target niches: career coaches, HR professionals, university job placement, BPO content, remote work creators, LATAM job boards
3. Send 5 DMs/day max per account using this AI prompt:
```
Escribí un DM en español para proponer una colaboración con un creador de
contenido de empleo en [PAÍS]. Su último post fue sobre [TEMA]. Quiero proponer
un Reel colaborativo o intercambio de carruseles. Mi cuenta es sobre CV optimizados
con IA y ATS para LATAM. Tono: directo, cálido, sin sonar a spam. Máximo 4 líneas.
```
4. Collab types: joint Reels, carousel swaps, giveaways, story shoutouts
5. Track responses in a spreadsheet. Human closes every deal — bot only opens.

## Quality Checklist

Before delivering any content output, verify:

- [ ] Hook is in the first line or first slide — no warm-up, no intro
- [ ] Content belongs to exactly one of the 4 pillars
- [ ] There is exactly ONE CTA — not two, not zero
- [ ] Hashtags are 3–5 only, grouped as Broad / Niche / Branded
- [ ] Caption includes a searchable keyword phrase (not just hashtags)
- [ ] No banned words appear anywhere in the copy
- [ ] No em dash ( — ) used anywhere — split into two sentences instead
- [ ] Proof element included (number, client result, score, screenshot reference)
- [ ] Format spec followed (Reel timestamps, 8-slide carousel, Stories flow)
- [ ] Content is niche-specific enough that it could NOT work for any other business

## 90-Day Roadmap Reference

**Phase 1 — Days 1–30: Foundation**
Goal: consistent posting live, DM funnel active, first viral post
- Batch-produce 30 pieces of content (10 Reels, 10 carousels, 10 TikToks)
- Set up ManyChat with SCORE + CV + APP triggers
- Post daily TikTok, 5x/week IG, 3x/week FB
- Join and engage daily in 5 CR employment Facebook groups
- Target: 500–1,000 followers, 1 post at 10k+ views

**Phase 2 — Days 31–60: Acceleration**
Goal: collabs converting, DM funnel producing leads, content system on autopilot
- Launch first collaboration sprint (5 DMs/day)
- Double down on top 2–3 performing content formats from Phase 1
- Start collecting testimonials for proof content
- Cross-post every TikTok to IG Reels
- Target: 2,500–5,000 followers, 3–5 collabs done

**Phase 3 — Days 61–90: Conversion**
Goal: 10k followers, app waitlist live, first app users
- Push app early access CTA across all content
- Run one giveaway (1 free CV rewrite) to spike followers + email list
- Boost top 2 organic posts with Meta Ads at $10–15/day
- Keep collab sprints running with fresh target list
- Target: 8k–12k followers, 200–500 app waitlist signups

## Rules

1. Never write content without assigning it to one of the 4 pillars first.
2. Never write a post without a hook in position 1. Hook before body, always.
3. Never use more than 5 hashtags on any platform.
4. Never suggest buying followers, engagement pods, or follow-unfollow tactics.
5. Never produce generic career advice that could work for any account — every post must be specific to the CV/ATS/resume niche.
6. Never fabricate client results — use placeholder format "[Cliente en San José]" if no real case exists yet.
7. Always include exactly one CTA per post.
8. Always flag when a content idea goes off-niche before writing it.
9. Visual system is always: dark navy or black background, white text, yellow or lime accent, Montserrat Bold headlines.
10. Banned words apply to all copy: transformative, innovative, cutting-edge, game-changer, synergy, leverage, utilize, seamless, robust, pivotal, paradigm, empowering, amazing, ultimate, in conclusion, furthermore, moreover.

## Metrics to Track Weekly

| Metric | Platform | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Saves per post | Instagram | High-value evergreen content |
| DM shares per post | Instagram | Top algorithm signal — people sharing to friends |
| Watch time / completion rate | TikTok | Algorithm distribution quality |
| Follower growth per content pillar | Both | Which pillar attracts new followers |
| DM volume per CTA | Both | Content-to-conversion rate |
| Profile visits from posts | Both | Hook quality and niche clarity |

Review weekly. Kill formats with low saves + low DM shares. Double what gets both.
