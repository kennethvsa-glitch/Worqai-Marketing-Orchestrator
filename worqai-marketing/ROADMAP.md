# WorqAI Marketing Roadmap — 90 Days

This is the living marketing plan. Update monthly as metrics come in.

## North Star Metric

**Weekly Active Resume Completions (WARC)** — users who start AND finish a resume per week. See `.claude/skills/analytics-kpi/SKILL.md` for full framework.

## 90-Day Goals (Working Targets)

| Metric | Day 30 | Day 60 | Day 90 |
|---|---|---|---|
| WARC (weekly) | 50 | 300 | 800 |
| Sign-ups (cumulative) | 500 | 3,000 | 10,000 |
| Paid conversions | 5 | 50 | 200 |
| MRR | $45 | $450 | $2,000 |
| Email subscribers | 200 | 1,500 | 5,000 |
| Blog posts published | 8 | 24 | 48 |
| pSEO pages live | 50 | 150 | 300 |

These are guesses. Adjust after Week 2 data.

---

## Phase 1: Validate (Days 1-30)

**Theme:** Don't ship features. Ship conversations. Find out who this is really for.

### Goals
- 15-20 ICP interviews completed
- Landing page + waitlist live
- First 200 email subscribers
- Pre-launch audience of 500+ across channels

### Workstreams

**Strategy**
- Run 15-20 user interviews (use `customer-interviews` skill)
- Write positioning statement (April Dunford framework)
- Define pricing hypothesis
- Set up analytics stack (Plausible + PostHog)

**Content**
- Kenneth's founder LinkedIn: 3 posts/week minimum
- Reddit: 2 helpful posts/week in target subs (no promo)
- Weekly newsletter to waitlist
- Launch WorqAI Instagram page

**Ads**
- No paid spend yet. Organic only.

**Product (cofounder's side)**
- Functional sign-up flow
- Resume builder MVP
- ATS download in Spanish
- Free tier + watermark system

---

## Phase 2: Launch (Days 31-60)

**Theme:** Coordinated hit across 3+ channels same week. Get to 3,000 users.

### Goals
- Product Hunt launch (top 10 of the day)
- Hacker News Show HN post
- First paid ad campaigns on Meta
- 3,000 total sign-ups

### Workstreams

**Strategy**
- Run `/project:launch-plan` and execute
- Post-launch analysis + iteration plan

**Content**
- Launch blog post (2000+ words, bilingual)
- 20 pSEO pages live for long-tail keywords
- Press/community outreach
- Daily social updates during launch week

**Ads**
- Start Meta ads: $20/day for 2 weeks, iterate creatives weekly
- Test 3 carousel ad concepts (different design systems, different hooks)
- Track CAC — kill ads above $30 CAC
- Scale ads under $15 CAC to $50/day

**Email**
- Welcome sequence live (5 emails)
- Launch announcement to waitlist
- Free → Paid conversion sequence live

---

## Phase 3: Optimize (Days 61-90)

**Theme:** Double down on what works. Kill what doesn't. Build systems for compounding.

### Goals
- Identify top 2 acquisition channels
- MRR $2,000+
- Churn under 8%/month
- D7 retention above 25%

### Workstreams

**Strategy**
- Monthly cohort analysis
- Kill decisions: which channels to stop, which to scale
- Start referral program design (see `referral-program` skill)
- Hire first growth contractor if budget allows

**Content**
- Scale pSEO to 300 pages
- First pillar content piece (3000+ words)
- Monthly newsletter rhythm locked
- First user case studies (permissioned)

**Ads**
- Scale winning creatives to $100-200/day
- Retargeting campaigns for non-converters
- Lookalike audiences built from paid users

**Growth**
- Reddit AMA in one major sub
- Partnership outreach (bootcamps, career coaches)
- Referral program soft launch to top 100 users

---

## Monthly Review Template

At the end of every month, write to `roadmap/review_{month}.md`:

1. **What we shipped** (features, content, campaigns)
2. **Metrics vs targets** (WARC, sign-ups, conversions, MRR)
3. **What worked** (double down candidates)
4. **What didn't** (kill candidates)
5. **What we learned** (new insights about ICP, channels, product)
6. **Next month's focus** (1-3 bets, not 10)

---

## Things Not On The Roadmap (Yet)

Deliberately cut for focus. Revisit quarterly:

- LinkedIn paid ads (test only after Meta ads are profitable)
- TikTok organic (great for LATAM but big time sink)
- Affiliate program (too early — need >1000 paid users)
- Spanish podcast sponsorships (test after finding PMF)
- YouTube channel (high production cost)
- Localized versions per country (Mexico, Argentina etc — do after $10k MRR)
