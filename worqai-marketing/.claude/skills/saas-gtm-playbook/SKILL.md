# SaaS GTM Playbook

## The 5-Pillar Framework

Every GTM decision for WorqAI ladders up to one of these:

1. **ICP validation** — Who exactly? Backed by interviews, not assumptions.
2. **Positioning + messaging** — What do we say, in what words, to whom?
3. **Pricing + packaging** — Free tier vs paid vs one-time. Hybrid wins in 2026.
4. **Channel mix** — Where do we reach them? No single-channel playbooks work.
5. **Metrics that matter** — Track activation, retention, CAC payback — not vanity.

## WorqAI GTM Motion

WorqAI is a **product-led growth (PLG)** motion because:
- Low ACV (under $100/year per user)
- Fast time-to-value (resume done in 10 min)
- Self-serve friendly (no sales call needed)
- Freemium converts naturally as users hit limits

**North Star metric:** Weekly Active Resume Completions (WARC) — users who start AND finish a resume per week. This captures both acquisition and activation in one number.

**Secondary metrics:**
- Activation rate: % of sign-ups who complete a resume in their first session
- D7 retention: % still active 7 days after sign-up
- Free → Paid conversion rate (target: 3-5% for freemium B2C SaaS)
- MRR growth rate (target: 15-20% MoM in first 6 months)
- CAC payback period (target: under 6 months)

## ICP Validation Process

Don't build more features. Talk to 15-20 users. For each interview capture:
- **Context**: Current job situation, why searching now, last time they updated CV
- **Jobs-to-be-done**: What they're trying to accomplish (interview? career change? remote?)
- **Current alternatives**: What tools/methods they use today, what frustrates them
- **Willingness to pay**: What have they paid for similar tools? What would make $9/mo feel cheap?
- **Language/region**: Are they using Spanish, English, or bilingual? LATAM country?

**Output:** A 1-page ICP doc with 3 user personas, their top 3 pains, and their exact language for describing those pains (use that language in copy).

## Positioning Framework (April Dunford)

Fill this out:
- **Competitive alternatives:** What would users use if WorqAI didn't exist?
- **Unique attributes:** What do we do that nobody else does?
- **Value (what you enable):** The outcome users get from those attributes
- **Best customer:** Who values those attributes most?
- **Market category:** How do users mentally file us?

For WorqAI:
- Alternatives: Rezi/Teal (English-only), ChatGPT (slow/slop), Word templates (dated)
- Attributes: Bilingual ES/EN + LATAM conventions + AI-assisted + ATS-safe
- Value: A recruiter-ready resume in 10 minutes for LATAM job seekers
- Best customer: LATAM professionals applying to remote/US roles
- Category: AI resume builder for LATAM

## Pricing Strategy

**2026 best practice:** Hybrid pricing (freemium + paid tiers + one-time) beats single-model pricing 85% of the time for consumer SaaS.

WorqAI pricing:
- **Free tier** — 1 download/month, watermarked. Purpose: acquisition + SEO signals
- **Pro $9/mo or $79/yr** — Unlimited + bilingual + LinkedIn. Purpose: recurring revenue
- **One-time $19** — Single polished resume. Purpose: convert users who hate subscriptions
- **Service $149-399** — Done-for-you via Profile Pro LATAM. Purpose: high-ticket upsell

**Anchor pricing by showing the yearly price as the default** ("$79/yr = $6.58/mo" reads cheaper than "$9/mo").

## Channel Strategy — First 90 Days

**Weeks 1-4: Validate**
- Interview 15-20 target users
- Build landing page + waitlist
- Post in 5 target Reddit communities (genuinely, not promo)
- Kenneth starts founder-led LinkedIn content (3 posts/week)

**Weeks 5-8: Launch**
- Product Hunt launch
- Hacker News Show HN post
- Instagram/Facebook organic (leverage existing Profile Pro LATAM audience)
- First paid ads on Meta (small test, $10-20/day)

**Weeks 9-12: Optimize**
- Double down on channels that worked
- Kill channels that didn't
- Start SEO content (long-tail pSEO pages)
- Email nurture for waitlist + free users

## Launch Sequence

Each launch should hit 3-5 channels same day for coordinated push:
1. Product Hunt (schedule for Tuesday 12:01am PT)
2. Hacker News Show HN (same day)
3. Reddit in target subs (different subs, different angles)
4. LinkedIn (Kenneth's founder post)
5. Twitter/X (if we build presence)
6. Email to waitlist
7. Instagram carousel + story

## KPIs Benchmarks (B2C SaaS 2026)

| Metric | Bad | OK | Good | Great |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free→Paid conversion | <1% | 2% | 4% | 6%+ |
| Monthly churn | >10% | 7% | 5% | <3% |
| CAC payback | >12mo | 9mo | 6mo | <4mo |
| LTV:CAC | <1:1 | 2:1 | 3:1 | 4:1+ |
| D7 retention | <20% | 30% | 40% | 50%+ |

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Treating marketing as a standalone function.** GTM = product + pricing + marketing + sales. All must align.
2. **Acquiring before activating.** Fix the resume-completion funnel before scaling ads.
3. **Building features before validating ICP.** Talk to users first.
4. **Chasing vanity metrics.** Followers ≠ revenue. Track activation and retention.
5. **Single-channel dependency.** Even if Reddit works, build email + SEO + ads in parallel.
6. **Generic founder-led content.** "My journey building WorqAI" is boring. "Here's why LATAM resumes fail ATS systems" is useful.
