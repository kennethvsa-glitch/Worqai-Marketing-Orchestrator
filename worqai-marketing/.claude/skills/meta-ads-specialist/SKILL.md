## What This Skill Does

Plans and writes Facebook and Instagram ad campaigns that look and read like human-crafted agency work. Every output starts from a real customer moment, a single promise, and a 3-layer message hierarchy — not a template. Covers hooks, static and video ad copy, visual briefs, AI image prompts, funnels, campaign configuration, and anti-slop review.

Primary service context: CV optimization and LinkedIn profile services for Spanish-speaking professionals in Costa Rica and LATAM. The principles apply to any service business.

- User asks to write a Facebook or Instagram ad, hook, or headline
- User needs a full campaign funnel (lead magnet → audit → paid service)
- User asks for a visual brief or static ad description (Canva, text-overlay, before/after)
- User needs an AI image prompt that won't look like AI slop
- User wants campaign setup: objective, targeting, budget, ad structure
- User says "revisa este ad" or asks why their ad isn't converting
- User asks for carousel copy, Stories script, or Reel script for Meta

## Inputs Required

Before writing any copy or visual brief, collect:

1. **Service or product** — what is being sold (CV rewrite, LinkedIn audit, full package, other)
2. **Target audience** — who specifically (age, location, job situation, pain point)
3. **Goal of the ad** — leads, messages to WhatsApp, free audit signups, direct sale
4. **Any existing copy or visual** — to audit or use as a base
5. **Language** — Spanish (default for Costa Rica/LATAM) or English

If inputs 1–3 are missing, ask for them before writing. Do not produce generic copy without a customer moment.

## Step-by-Step Workflow

### Step 1 — Define the Customer Moment

Before writing a single word, answer these four questions in plain language:

1. Who is the specific person? (age, location, job situation right now)
2. What is happening in their day when they see this ad?
3. What is the exact frustration they are feeling at that moment?
4. What is the ONE specific shift this ad promises?

Example output for CV/LinkedIn service:
> "María, 29, bilingüe, Heredia. Llevan 6 semanas enviando CVs sin respuesta. Son las 9pm, está en Instagram, acaba de ver que la empresa donde aplicó ya contrató. Frustrada, empieza a pensar que el problema es ella. La promesa: después de revisar su LinkedIn headline, los reclutadores la van a encontrar en búsqueda."

Do not skip this step. Generic ads come from skipping this step.

### Step 2 — Choose the Hook Type

Select one hook type based on the customer moment. Match the hook to the emotional state identified in Step 1.

| Hook Type | When to Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Result | Audience wants proof it works | "Pasé de 0 a 4 mensajes de reclutadores en una semana." |
| Question | Audience suspects a problem but can't name it | "¿Sabes por qué tu CV nunca llega a RRHH aunque apliques a 50 trabajos?" |
| Contrarian | Audience is doing something that isn't working | "Mandar CVs a 100 empresas es una pérdida de tiempo." |
| Curiosity | Audience wants the answer to a specific gap | "Hay una sola línea en tu LinkedIn que los reclutadores ven primero..." |
| Negative/Loss | Audience is risk-averse, fears wasted effort | "Para de mandar tu CV a ciegas. Hay un sistema que lo rechaza antes de que alguien lo lea." |

The hook is the first 3 seconds of video or the dominant text on a static ad. Everything else supports it.

### Step 3 — Write the Ad (3-Layer Hierarchy)

Every ad — video, static, carousel, Story — has exactly three layers:

**Layer 1 — Hook (scroll-stopper):** The customer moment in one line. Named frustration, specific result, or pattern interrupt.

**Layer 2 — Support (proof or problem framing):** One micro-story, one concrete number, or one specific before/after. Not a feature list.

**Layer 3 — CTA (one action, one destination):** "Escríbeme AUDITORÍA por DM" or "Link en bio para la revisión gratis." Never two CTAs.

### Step 4 — Run the Anti-Slop Check

Before delivering any copy, verify:

- Read it out loud. If you wouldn't say it to a friend, rewrite it.
- Swap the service name for a competitor's. If the ad still makes sense, it's too generic — add a specific detail.
- Count concrete elements: numbers, time frames, locations, real situations. Minimum two per ad.
- Scan for banned words (list in Rules section). If any appear, replace with plain language.

### Step 5 — Deliver the Output

For **ad copy**: hook + body + CTA, labeled by layer.
For **video scripts**: timestamps — Hook (0–3s) / Story (3–20s) / CTA (20–30s).
For **static ad briefs**: background, headline text, subtext, CTA placement, dimensions.
For **AI image prompts**: subject + setting + platform/format + photography preamble + brand cues + negative constraints.
For **campaign setup**: objective, targeting, budget, ad count, test duration.

## Output Formats

### Ad Copy Template
```
HOOK (Layer 1):
[One line — named frustration, result, or pattern interrupt]

BODY (Layer 2):
[One micro-story or one proof element — 2–4 sentences max]

CTA (Layer 3):
[One action — "Escríbeme AUDITORÍA por DM" or equivalent]
```

### Video Script Template (15–30 seconds)
```
0–3s  | HOOK: [scroll-stopping line or visual — on-screen text]
3–15s | PROBLEM + SOLUTION: [real problem in 1 sentence + your fix in 1 sentence]
15–25s| PROOF: [screenshot, before/after, specific number]
25–30s| CTA: [one action only]
```

### Static Ad Brief Template (Canva)
```
FORMAT: [1080x1350px 4:5 / 1080x1920px 9:16 / 1080x1080px 1:1]
BACKGROUND: [dark navy / black / specific color]
HEADLINE (30–40% of image): [hook text, bold sans-serif]
SUBTEXT (secondary): [support text, regular weight]
CTA STRIP (bottom 20%): [action text + logo/name]
STYLE NOTES: [contrast level, no gradients, no blobs, etc.]
```

### AI Image Prompt Template
```
SUBJECT: [who, what they're doing, their emotional state]
SETTING: [real specific place — not a studio, not a luxury office]
PLATFORM: [Instagram feed ad, 4:5 vertical / Stories 9:16]
STYLE: [Sony A7R IV, 35mm f/1.8, natural light / iPhone photo, casual, real apartment]
BRAND CUES: [colors, logo placement if any]
NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS: no distorted hands, no extra fingers, no neon gradients, no floating 3D blobs, no surreal lighting, no text in background, avoid: cinematic, ultra detailed, hyperreal
```

### Campaign Setup Template
```
OBJECTIVE: [Leads / Messages to WhatsApp]
AUDIENCE: [24–45 years, Costa Rica, no specific interests — broad]
BUDGET: [$10–15/day total]
STRUCTURE: 1 campaign → 1 ad set → 3–5 ads (same body, different hooks)
TEST DURATION: 72 hours
KILL THRESHOLD: thumb-stop rate under 25%
SCALE RULE: double budget every 48–72h on winner, same ad set
```

## Examples

### Example 1 — Pain Point Ad (Spanish, WhatsApp objective)

**Customer moment:** Job seeker in Costa Rica, 6 weeks of applications, zero responses, seeing the ad at night on Instagram.

**Before (AI slop version):**
> "¿Buscas trabajo? Nuestro servicio innovador y transformador te ayuda a optimizar tu CV y LinkedIn de manera efectiva para maximizar tu potencial profesional. ¡Contáctanos hoy!"

**After (anti-slop version):**
```
HOOK: "Más del 70% de CVs son rechazados antes de que un humano los vea."

BODY: "No porque el candidato no sea bueno. Sino porque el formato confunde al sistema. Si llevas semanas aplicando sin respuesta, el problema probablemente no es tu experiencia. Es cómo está estructurado tu CV."

CTA: "Te hago una revisión gratis esta semana. Escríbeme 'AUDITORÍA' por DM."
```

**Why it works:** Named frustration ("semanas aplicando sin respuesta"), specific proof stat (70%), reframes the problem without blaming the reader, one clear action.

---

### Example 2 — Micro-Story Ad (Result hook)

**Customer moment:** Professional who has seen others get recruiter messages on LinkedIn but never received one.

**Before:**
> "Mejora tu perfil de LinkedIn con nuestros expertos y consigue más visibilidad con reclutadores. Servicio profesional, resultados garantizados."

**After:**
```
HOOK: "Mi cliente llevaba 3 meses buscando trabajo. Cambié su headline de LinkedIn y en 5 días tenía 2 entrevistas."

BODY: "Un cambio. Un campo. Eso fue todo."

CTA: "Si quieres saber qué dice el tuyo y por qué los reclutadores no te están encontrando, escríbeme."
```

**Why it works:** Specific timeframe (3 meses, 5 días), specific outcome (2 entrevistas), no features mentioned, ends with curiosity pull, not a hard sell.

## Quality Checklist

Before delivering any output, verify:

- [ ] Customer moment was defined before writing (specific person, day, frustration, promise)
- [ ] Hook names a specific frustration or result — not a category or feature
- [ ] Body contains at minimum one concrete detail: number, time frame, location, or real situation
- [ ] CTA is one action to one destination — not two options
- [ ] Copy read aloud sounds like a real person talking, not a marketing brief
- [ ] Service name can NOT be swapped for a competitor without the ad falling apart
- [ ] Zero banned words in the final copy (see Rules)
- [ ] No gradient buttons, colored icon circles, or 3-column feature grids in visual briefs
- [ ] AI image prompts include photography preamble AND negative constraints
- [ ] Campaign setup includes objective, broad targeting, budget, ad count, kill threshold
- [ ] Default language is Spanish unless user explicitly requested English

## Rules

**Hard constraints:**
1. Never write copy without first completing Step 1 (customer moment). Ask if missing.
2. Never produce a visual brief that includes: floating 3D blobs, neon gradients, colored side borders on cards, icon-in-a-circle features, centered layout on everything, or wavy dividers.
3. Never use these words in any copy output: transformative, innovative, cutting-edge, game-changer, revolutionary, robust, seamless, leverage, utilize, facilitate, synergy, paradigm shift, empower, unlock your potential, all-in-one solution, amazing, ultimate, world-class, streamline, dynamic, impactful, groundbreaking.
4. Every video script uses the 3-act structure with timestamps. No exceptions.
5. Every static ad brief includes dimensions and format ratio.
6. Every AI image prompt includes a photography-style preamble and the full negative constraints block.

**Market context (Costa Rica / LATAM):**
- Facebook: 4.6M+ users in Costa Rica, 25–34 is the core segment
- WhatsApp CTA converts better than landing pages — use "Mensajes" objective
- $10–15/day is enough to test with real data in 72 hours
- Hyper-local callouts work: "en Costa Rica", "zona franca", "profesionales bilingües"
- CPMs are lower than Mexico/Colombia — broad targeting works well here
- This market responds to ads that feel like a real person, not corporate advertising
- Facebook Groups for employment are high-ROI organic: answer questions, offer DM audit

**Anti-slop visual rules:**
- Dark navy (#0a0f1e) or black + white text + one accent (yellow or lime green) for urgency
- Two fonts max: one bold sans-serif for hooks (Montserrat Bold, Inter Black, Poppins Bold), one regular for body
- Hook text occupies 30–40% of image space
- 4:5 format (1080x1350px) is the default starting point for feed ads
- Solid or simple backgrounds outperform busy photo backgrounds
- Never add decorative elements that carry no information

## Testing Guide

Use these prompts to verify the Skill is triggering and producing correct output:

1. `"Escríbeme un ad de Facebook para vender mi servicio de revisión de CV. El objetivo es mensajes a WhatsApp."` → Should ask for customer moment info if not provided, then produce hook + body + CTA in Spanish with anti-slop check passed.

2. `"Dame 5 hooks para un Reel de auditoría gratuita de LinkedIn para profesionales en Costa Rica."` → Should produce 5 hooks, one per type (result, question, contrarian, curiosity, negative), all with concrete specific details.

3. `"Necesito un prompt de imagen para generar un ad estático que no parezca IA."` → Should output full prompt with subject, setting, platform, photography preamble, brand cues, and complete negative constraints block.

4. `"Revisa este copy y dime si suena como IA: 'Nuestro servicio innovador transforma tu perfil profesional para maximizar tu potencial.'"` → Should identify slop indicators (innovador, transforma, maximizar tu potencial, no concrete detail, no customer moment) and rewrite.
