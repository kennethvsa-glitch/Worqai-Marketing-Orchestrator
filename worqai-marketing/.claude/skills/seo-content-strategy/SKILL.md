# SEO Content Strategy — WorqAI

## Why SEO Matters for WorqAI

Resume builder is a **high-search-intent category**. People search "cómo hacer un CV", "resume template free", "best resume builder 2026" — and they convert at higher rates than average SaaS because the intent is transactional.

2026 data: Organic search drives 30-60% of B2C SaaS pipeline. Payback is 6-12 months — slow but compounding. Every article published keeps earning traffic forever.

## Strategy: Two Funnels, Two Languages

**Spanish funnel (priority — our wedge):**
- Long-tail, low-competition keywords in Spanish
- Target LATAM Google + US Hispanic Google
- Each piece links to sign-up with ES default

**English funnel (later — more competition):**
- Only target bilingual/remote-specific queries where LATAM angle differentiates
- e.g., "resume for US companies from Latin America", not "best resume builder"

## Keyword Research Approach

### Where to find keywords
- **Google autocomplete** (Spanish): Type "cv para" and capture suggestions
- **People Also Ask** boxes on competitor articles
- **Reddit/Quora threads** — actual user language
- **Answer The Public** (free tier) for question-based queries
- **Ahrefs / Semrush** (if budget) for volume + difficulty

### Keywords to prioritize
- Long-tail (3+ words) — lower competition, higher intent
- Question-based (cómo, qué, cuál) — AI search friendly
- Location-modified — "cv para trabajo en mexico", "resume para chile remoto"
- Problem-focused — "mi cv no pasa el ats", "cv rechazado sin respuesta"

### Keywords to avoid
- "Best resume builder" (too competitive, English-only wedge lost)
- Single words ("resume", "cv") — informational, non-transactional
- Branded competitor terms (bad user experience)

## Content Types (Priority Order)

### 1. Problem-led blog posts (highest ROI)
Write about pain before the solution. Example titles:
- "Por qué tu CV no está llegando a entrevistas (y no es tu culpa)"
- "El error del CV que hace que el ATS te rechace automáticamente"
- "How LATAM applicants lose 70% of US remote roles (and fix it)"

Each post: 1200-1800 words, 1 target keyword + 3-5 related, CTA to free tier.

### 2. Programmatic SEO (pSEO) pages
Generate landing pages at scale for long-tail queries. Template:
- `/cv-para-[profesion]` — "CV para desarrollador", "CV para diseñador", "CV para marketing"
- `/resume-for-[role]-from-[country]` — "Resume for developer from Mexico"
- `/plantilla-cv-[industria]` — "Plantilla CV tech", "Plantilla CV salud"

Each page: 400-600 words, example CV, CTA to use that template in WorqAI. Goal: 100+ pages covering long-tail in 60 days.

### 3. Comparison pages
Transactional intent, high conversion.
- "WorqAI vs Rezi: cuál es mejor para LATAM"
- "ChatGPT vs WorqAI para hacer un CV"
- "Mejores resume builders 2026"

### 4. Guide/pillar content
Long-form (3000+ words), authoritative, high dwell time.
- "Guía completa: cómo hacer un CV que pase ATS en 2026"
- "The LATAM Professional's Guide to Remote Work Applications"

Each pillar links to 5-10 cluster posts (types 1-3 above).

## Writing Principles

- **Hook in line 1.** No preamble. "Si tu CV tiene fotos, 80% de los ATS lo rechazan automáticamente."
- **Short paragraphs** (2-3 sentences max). Mobile readers scan.
- **H2 every 200-300 words.** Structure scannable.
- **Lists and tables** for comparison/step content.
- **Real examples** with before/after.
- **Internal links** to 2-3 related posts + 1 sign-up CTA.
- **One clear CTA** — try WorqAI free, nothing else.

## AI Search Optimization (2026)

Google's SGE + Perplexity + ChatGPT search are pulling answers from content. To rank in AI results:
- Answer the question in the first 100 words
- Use clear H2s phrased as questions
- Include a concise TL;DR near the top
- Use definition-style sentences ("An ATS is...")
- Structured data (FAQ schema) on Q&A content

## Publishing Cadence

**Minimum:** 2 posts/week (1 pillar/cluster, 1 pSEO batch).
**Ideal:** 4 posts/week + 20 pSEO pages/week for first 3 months.

Consistency beats volume. Pick a rhythm you can sustain for 6+ months.

## Measurement

Track per article:
- Organic impressions (Search Console)
- Organic clicks + CTR
- Ranking position for target keyword
- Time on page + scroll depth
- Conversions (sign-ups from that page)

Monthly review: Kill posts ranking below position 30 after 90 days. Double down on posts in positions 5-15 (lift them to top 3 with updates/backlinks).
