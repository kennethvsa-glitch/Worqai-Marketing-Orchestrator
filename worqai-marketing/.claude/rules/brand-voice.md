# Brand Voice

## WorqAI Voice

**English:** Direct, warm, slightly irreverent. Talk like a helpful friend who happens to know recruiting. Never corporate. Never hype. Short sentences. Concrete examples over abstractions.

**Español:** Cercano, directo, sin formalidad exagerada. Tuteo por defecto (no "usted"). Nada de español neutro telenovelesco — tono latinoamericano natural, modismos leves donde ayudan ("listo", "ya", "dale").

## Voice Principles

- **Specificity over vagueness.** "43% de los CVs latinos fallan ATS" beats "muchos CVs tienen problemas".
- **Pain-first.** Name the pain the reader feels right now. Then offer help.
- **Short paragraphs.** 2-3 sentences. Mobile readers scan.
- **No hedging.** "Creemos que tal vez..." → "Esto es lo que funciona:".
- **Numbers over adjectives.** "2,847 usuarios" beats "miles de usuarios".

## Never

- Corporate-speak ("at scale", "best-in-class", "industry-leading")
- Stock-photo language ("team of diverse professionals collaborating")
- Fake enthusiasm ("We're SO excited to announce...")
- Manufactured urgency ("Only 3 spots left!" when there are 100)
- Generic AI voice ("I'd be happy to help you with that")

## Language Decision Tree

- Content targeting LATAM general audience → Spanish
- Content about applying to US companies → Bilingual (Spanish primary, English examples)
- Technical docs / internal planning → English
- WorqAI product UI → Bilingual toggle
