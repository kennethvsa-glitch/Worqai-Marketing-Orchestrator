# Organic Growth Plan — Pre-Launch (8 Weeks)

**Status:** Active · App launches in ~2 months
**Focus:** Carousels first. Videos after. No paid spend yet.

---

## The Framework: Audience Before Product

Don't promote an app that doesn't exist yet. Build an audience around the pain the app solves. When launch day comes, you're not starting from zero — you're activating people who already trust you.

**Two-phase split:**

- **Weeks 1–6 (now):** Pure value. 0% product mentions. 100% "this person knows their shit about CVs/job hunting."
- **Weeks 7–8 (pre-launch):** Start teasing. Waitlist. Behind the scenes. "Something's coming."

---

## Current Channel Priority

Platforms active: Instagram, Facebook, TikTok.

**Now (weeks 1–6):** Carousels only. 1 post every 3 days. Don't dump all 5 at once.
**Later (weeks 7+):** Add video once carousel rhythm is locked in and budget allows for video tools.

### Carousel types to build next

Current carousels are all **"education + mistake correction"** format. Need more variety. Proven carousel approaches to rotate:

1. **Listicle** — "5 cosas que matan tu CV antes de que lo lea un humano" — numbered, fast, scannable
2. **Before/After** — show the bad version slide 1–3, the fixed version slide 4–6, the result slide 7
3. **Myth vs. Reality** — "Lo que creés vs. lo que pasa realmente" — high shareability
4. **Step-by-step how-to** — "Cómo optimizar tu CV para ATS en 4 pasos" — saves-magnet
5. **Data story** — one stat per slide, building a narrative (already partially done with salary carousel)
6. **Comparison** — side-by-side (LATAM CV vs. USA CV, bad recruiter post vs. good one)
7. **"Nadie te dice esto"** contrarian — opens with a truth that feels forbidden
8. **Quick win** — one actionable tip per slide, 7 tips, totally actionable
9. **Case study / story** — "Este CV pasó de 0 a 3 entrevistas en una semana — esto es lo que cambió"
10. **FAQ format** — "Preguntas que me hacen todo el tiempo sobre CVs"

Aim to have 2–3 carousel types rotating so the feed doesn't look like a single template repeated.

## Video Plan (parked — activate later)

UGC tools (Higgsfield, etc.) are monthly subscriptions — not worth it until the carousel content rhythm is proven and monetization is closer.

**When to activate video:**
- Carousel content is posting consistently for 4+ weeks
- Organic reach is building (saves, follows from carousels)
- App is 2–3 weeks from launch OR budget opens up

**Formats queued for when video activates:**
- "POV" format — `POV: mandaste tu CV a 40 trabajos y cero respuestas`
- "Lo que nadie te dice" hook — contrarian truth opener, high retention
- Screen-recording with voiceover — ATS demo, no face needed, highest-performing format in the niche
- React/duet to job postings — zero scripting, comment bait
- Founder story clip — 60 sec, Kenneth's real experience, repurposed everywhere

**UGC note when budget opens:** Keep AI video at 40% max. Mix in real screen recordings and Kenneth's face. LATAM audiences respond better to human-feeling content.

Scripts and text-to-video prompts for 3 videos are already written and saved. Ready to execute when the time comes.
