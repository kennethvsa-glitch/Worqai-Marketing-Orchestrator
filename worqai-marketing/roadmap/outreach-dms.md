## The Core Split

Two audiences. Two completely different pitches. Never mix them.

| | Track A — Agency | Track B — Founder/Creator |
|---|---|---|
| What they buy | Margin, capacity, relief | Status, autopilot, no Canva |
| Speed angle | YES — agencies buy speed and efficiency | NO — speed signals cheap to founders |
| CTA energy | Permission-based (evaluating a business relationship) | Command (they want someone decisive) |
| Framing | "Production partner behind your agency" | "Studio that handles your content" |

---

Name the attached carousel like a premium studio, not an export.

## TRACK A — Agency

### Cold DM (attach carousel, send directly)

**Spanish:**

> Hola [nombre]. Vi [post específico — ej: "su gráfico del 10 de marzo"] y lo pasé por mi sistema de producción visual.
>
> Les armé este carrusel en la marca de [agencia/cliente]. Se los dejo adjunto, 100% suyo para que lo publiquen.
>
> Lo hice con su propia marca para mostrarles el nivel que manejo. Mi negocio es actuar como motor de producción backend — 100% white-label — para los clientes de agencias como la suya.
>
> Básicamente elimino su cuello de botella de diseño. Proceso carruseles con esta calidad gráfica en menos de 48 horas, a una fracción de lo que cuesta cargar al equipo interno.
>
> Revisen el carrusel adjunto. Si les interesa esta velocidad de producción para las cuentas de sus clientes y aumentar su margen operativo, avísenme y hacemos una prueba.

**English:**

> Hey [name]. Saw [specific post] and ran it through my visual production system.
>
> Built this carousel in your brand. Attached — 100% yours to publish.
>
> I did it in your brand to show you the level I work at. My business is acting as a white-label production backend for agencies like yours.
>
> Basically I eliminate your design bottleneck. I process carousels at this quality level in under 48 hours, at a fraction of what it costs to load your internal team.
>
> Check the attached carousel. If you want this production speed for your client accounts and better operating margin, let me know and we'll run a trial.

**Tactical note:** Reference the specific post by name. Lead with the deliverable, not the pitch. White-label framing before pricing. CTA is a trial, not a call.
