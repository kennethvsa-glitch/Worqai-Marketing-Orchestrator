# Anti-Slop Rules

Applies to all copy written for motion graphics.

## Banned Words (English)

Never use: unlock, unleash, elevate, leverage, game-changer, cutting-edge, dive into, deep dive, let's explore, empower, transform, revolutionize, supercharge, seamless, robust, streamlined, holistic, take it to the next level.

## Banned Phrases (Spanish)

Never use: "¿Sabías que...?" como apertura, "En el mundo de hoy...", "En la era digital...", "libera tu potencial", "transforma tu carrera", "desbloquea", "potencia tu".

## Instead Use

- Specific numbers and results.
- Concrete before/after comparisons.
- Direct language ("This does X" not "This empowers you to X").
- Real examples from the client's industry.

## Banned Implied Claims (Spanish — no source_facts provided)

### Scarcity Without Data
Never use: "solo X segundos" unsourced, "muy pocos candidatos", "casi nadie pasa"

### Vague Consensus
Never use: "es bien sabido que", "todo el mundo sabe", "lo que buscan los reclutadores"

### Causal Claims Without Mechanism
Never use: "esto hace que el reclutador confíe", "activa el filtro ATS", "mejora tu visibilidad automáticamente"

## Motion-Specific Copy Rules

- **Counter stats must be numeric.** If the stat field feeds a counter effect, it must be a number (e.g. `73` not `"73%"` — the `%` is appended by the template). Non-numeric counter input crashes the animation with a blank frame.
- **Kicker ≤ 6 words.** Motion kickers are timed to appear before the main stat — long kickers break the GSAP timeline timing.
- **Context ≤ 12 words.** Anything longer will overflow the safe zone on 9:16.
- **Source line is always present.** Even if it's "Dato interno WorqAI · 2025". Never omit.

## Voice Check (run before finalizing)

1. Would a real person say this in conversation?
2. Does it sound like a LinkedIn influencer? → Rewrite.
3. Can I cut 30% of adjectives and keep meaning? → Cut them.
4. Am I opening with a greeting/preamble? → Delete it. Start with the value.
