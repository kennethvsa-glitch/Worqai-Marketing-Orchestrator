# Motion Production Contract
## Approval Objects

- Creative specification: concept, audience, objective, promise, CTA, constraints.
- Storyboard: timestamped beats, copy, visual state, transition, audio cue.
- Candidate: exact Git diff plus render and QA evidence hashes.

Changing an approved object invalidates its downstream approvals.

## Required Draft Evidence

- validate-only output;
- preflight and orthography results;
- draft MP4;
- label-based contact sheet;
- safe-zone and frame findings;
- media probe with duration and streams;
- caption and cutdown inventory when requested.

## Review Outcomes

- `pass`: acceptance criteria met with no blocking findings.
- `repair`: bounded corrections are required.
- `blocked`: missing input, asset, authorization, or human decision.
