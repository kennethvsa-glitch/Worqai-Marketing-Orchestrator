# LinkedIn post — Free resume review

You should not have to keep rewriting your resume blindly.

A friend and I built WorqAI because we kept running into the same problem:

You can tell your resume is not working—but still have no idea what to fix first.

So this week, we are opening up free resume reviews.

No charge. No card. No sales call.

We will send you a short, practical diagnosis covering:

- what kind of role your resume currently appears to target;
- which strong experience is buried under vague wording;
- the first three things we would improve for the role you actually want.

We will not invent achievements or promise that a few edits will guarantee an interview.

The goal is simpler: help you understand what is unclear before you send another application.

Even if you never pay for WorqAI, we want you to leave with a clearer next step.

If you want a free review, comment **REVIEW** + the role you are applying for.

Please do not post your resume, email address, or any personal information in the comments. We will message you privately with the next step and reply in order.
