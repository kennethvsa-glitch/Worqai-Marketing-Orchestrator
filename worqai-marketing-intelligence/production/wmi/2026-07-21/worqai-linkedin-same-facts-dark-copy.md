# Dark LinkedIn visual — final copy

## Headline

Same facts. Different spotlight.

## Supporting line

A tailored resume does not create new evidence. It decides which real evidence leads.

## Illustrative focus map

Target role: Customer Support

The master resume contains several pieces of experience. The role spotlight brings three relevant, verifiable details forward:

- handled customer inquiries by email;
- logged incidents in Zendesk;
- followed cases through resolution.

Only use details you can verify. The example is illustrative.

## Rule and CTA

Move the spotlight. Not the truth.

Compare your resume with the role before you apply.

First analysis + first tailored resume free. No card required.

Illustrative example. No ATS or interview guarantee.

## Creative decision

This version intentionally avoids the light dashboard and before/after comparison used in earlier WorqAI graphics. It uses a dark editorial poster, a tilted paper resume, and a literal spotlight to show role-specific emphasis without implying that facts should be changed.
