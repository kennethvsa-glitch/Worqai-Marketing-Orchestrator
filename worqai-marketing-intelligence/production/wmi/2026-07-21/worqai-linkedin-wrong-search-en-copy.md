# LinkedIn post — English

Your resume can tell the truth and still answer the wrong search.

A role needs:

- email support
- Zendesk
- incident follow-up

Your resume says:

> Customer service and request handling.

You did the work. But the sentence makes the recruiter do the detective work.

Sending the same resume to every application feels efficient. The problem is that a generic resume often buries the evidence that is most relevant to a specific role.

Tailoring is not rewriting your career to please a machine.

It is choosing the most relevant facts you can prove—and describing them in language the employer will recognize.

If you really used Zendesk, handled email inquiries, and followed incidents through resolution, say that. If you did not, do not add it.
