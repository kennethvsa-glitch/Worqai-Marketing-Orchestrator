# Night Shift Proof — design review

## Why the first dark version was rejected

Two independent design audits found the same AI-SaaS patterns:

- thin borders and rounded cards;
- fake scanners, metrics, progress bars, browser chrome, and telemetry;
- neon-on-black with too many competing accents;
- decorative monospace labels and terminal syntax;
- one repeated dashboard composition across all seven slides;
- body copy that became too small at feed size;
- interface decoration replacing real evidence.

## Selected direction

**Night Shift Proof:** the carousel behaves like a résumé being edited on a dark desk.

The visual content is the actual work:

- torn résumé and job-description fragments;
- before/after language;
- thick proofreader arrows;
- red correction marks;
- one scarce lime approval gesture per slide;
- changing editorial rhythm instead of reusable cards.

## Design system

- Canvas: 1080 × 1350.
- Display: Bahnschrift SemiCondensed.
- Editorial note: Georgia Italic.
- Palette: matte ink `#080807`, warm paper `#F1EDE3`, graphite `#272924`, WorqAI lime `#B8FF00`, proof red `#FF5C57`.
- No cyan, glow, thin outlines, rounded cards, fake analytics, or pseudo-code.
- Smallest intentional copy: 20 px.
- Every slide has one dominant visual event and a different composition.

## Slide rhythm

1. Pure typographic hook interrupted by a vacancy strip.
2. Direct before/after sentence edit.
3. Minimal ethical statement.
4. Physical vacancy-to-evidence mapping.
5. Three full-width action bands.
6. Saveable checklist built around a giant cropped numeral.
7. Offer-led CTA with an oversized, circled `CV`.

## Validation

- Seven slides exported at 1080 × 1350.
- `tú` voice throughout; no `usted`.
- CTA included on slide 7: comment `CV` for one free month of WorqAI Pro.
- No rounded cards, fake metrics, progress bars, cyan accents, or glow effects.
- The redesign measures 55.46–78.86% pixel similarity to the rejected dashboard version, confirming a structural redesign rather than a cosmetic reskin.
