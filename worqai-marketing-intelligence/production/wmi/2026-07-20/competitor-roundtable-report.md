# WorqAI LinkedIn: competitive roundtable

Date: 2026-07-20  
Market: job-search and AI-resume tools  
Decision: replace the privacy-first flagship with a proof-led, explicitly illustrative CV teardown.

## Executive decision

The three-agent review reached the same ranking after cross-review:

1. **Before/after CV teardown:** best balance of useful engagement, product differentiation, trust, and qualified activation.
2. **Founder/community Pro offer:** likely to produce more raw comments, but only publish after capacity, eligibility, expiry, fulfillment, and attribution are confirmed.
3. **Privacy checklist:** useful nurture/trust content, but not the next acquisition post.

The supplied visual is an **illustrative example**, not a user result. That distinction is deliberate: no permissioned CV/vacancy pair was available, and the campaign must not imply a testimonial or outcome that did not occur.

There is no defensible basis for promising a “95% success post.” The evidence supports a testable hypothesis, not a guarantee.

## Platform evidence

- LinkedIn’s [Pages best-practices guide](https://business.linkedin.com/advertise/linkedin-pages/best-practices) says weekly company posting is associated with a 2× lift in engagement, images tend to receive about twice the comment rate, and video receives about five times the engagement. These are broad platform benchmarks, not guarantees for WorqAI.
- LinkedIn’s [analytics documentation](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a564051) exposes impressions, members reached, clicks, CTR, reactions, comments, reposts, follows, and engagement rate. Because engagement rate includes clicks, it should not be treated as conversion quality.
- Metricool’s [2026 LinkedIn study](https://metricool.com/linkedin-trends-study/) reports stronger average comment behavior from personal profiles and much higher interaction totals for document posts. “Interactions” include clicks and carousel navigation, so activation still needs separate measurement.
- Socialinsider’s [2026 benchmark](https://cdn.socialinsider.io/documents/linkedin_benchmarks_2026.pdf) similarly places native documents above single images for average page engagement in its dataset.
- LinkedIn supports Page boosts and Thought Leader Ads, but the [boosting options](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a10389112) should only be used after organic evidence shows the post is attracting the right behavior.

## Evidence versus inference

Observed:

- Competitor checklists often produce little visible discussion.
- Product demonstrations and specific teardowns attract more product questions.
- Free reviews and giveaways can produce very large comment counts.
- Huntr already published a privacy/anonymization offer close to the original WorqAI concept.

Inferred:

- A teardown should produce fewer raw comments than a giveaway but a higher share of relevant questions, saves, profile visits, and activated product sessions.
- Publishing from a founder profile and reposting from the company should create more human conversation than publishing only from the company page.
- A single illustrative example is production-safe now; a permissioned real case would be stronger and should become the follow-up document carousel.

## Final production brief

Visual message:

> ADAPTAR EL CV NO ES INVENTAR.  
> Es conectar lo que la vacante pide con experiencia que usted sí tiene.

Mechanism shown:

1. The vacancy asks for email support, Zendesk, and incident follow-up.
2. The illustrative candidate really did those three things.
3. A generic bullet is reorganized into a relevant, specific bullet without adding a tool, metric, responsibility, employer, or outcome.

Required guardrails:

- Label the example as fictitious/illustrative.
- Do not call it a success story or customer result.
- Do not claim that ATS systems reject all CVs automatically.
- Do not guarantee interviews, filtering outcomes, or employment.
- Do not publish the Pro promotion until fulfillment capacity and terms are confirmed.

## Comparison with existing WorqAI creative

The locally available prior WorqAI asset was also reviewed: `production/worqai-linkedin-first-post-slide.png`.

- The earlier creative is a dark, conversion-led launch card: “1 CV gratis / CV Score incluido,” a visible 84/100 score, feature checklist, and “Probar gratis” CTA.
- The new teardown deliberately does not repeat that structure. It is light, editorial, and proof-led. It shows one auditable transformation instead of another feature list.
- This gives the content sequence a clearer job: launch/offer → mechanism/proof → trust/privacy later.
- LinkedIn did not expose the WorqAI company feed or its live post counters through its public index during this research pass. Therefore this report does **not** claim to have verified the exact metrics of both live WorqAI posts. A screenshot or exported Page analytics would be needed for a precise internal comparison.

## Test and boost gate

Publish from a founder profile, then repost from the WorqAI page with original commentary. Measure at 24 and 72 hours:

- substantive comments per 1,000 impressions;
- saves and reposts;
- profile and company-page visits;
- link clicks with UTM;
- sign-ups;
- activated sessions: CV uploaded **and** vacancy pasted;
- completed audits/adaptations.

Do not boost immediately. Boost only if the post beats WorqAI’s own median on **substantive comments and activated sessions**, not just reactions. If the objective is site activation, use website visits/conversions rather than an engagement objective.
