# Benchmark Research Principles

Benchmark research is for pattern extraction, not copying.

Study:

- What the hook promises.
- How quickly the asset creates tension.
- What proof appears.
- How the narrative moves.
- What visual structure repeats.
- Where the CTA appears.
- Which audience assumption the asset relies on.

Never copy:

- Exact wording.
- Exact visual layouts.
- Unique claims.
- Proprietary examples.
- Brand-specific art direction.

Translate:

- Pattern into principle.
- Principle into WorqAI-native concept.
- Concept into production-ready asset.
