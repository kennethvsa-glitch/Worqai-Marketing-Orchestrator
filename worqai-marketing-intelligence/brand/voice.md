# WorqAI Voice

WorqAI should sound precise, confident, and commercially useful.

The voice is:

- Clear before clever.
- Specific before inspirational.
- Operational before theoretical.
- Professional without being stiff.
- Sharp without being mean.
- Human, not corporate.

Avoid sounding like generic AI SaaS. Prefer concrete before-and-after language,
visible proof, and practical outcomes.

Good WorqAI language:

- "Turn a generic resume into a role-specific application package."
- "Show the gap between the job description and the candidate's current resume."
- "Build the application around the role, not around a template."

Weak WorqAI language:

- "Unlock your career potential."
- "Revolutionize your job search."
- "Leverage AI for seamless success."
