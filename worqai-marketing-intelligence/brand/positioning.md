# WorqAI Positioning

WorqAI helps candidates turn scattered career material into targeted job-search
assets.

The current strongest positioning lane:

> AI-assisted job application intelligence for people who need sharper resumes,
> better fit signals, and more interviews.

Core promise:

> WorqAI helps people apply with documents that match the role instead of hoping
> a generic resume gets understood.

Primary buyers/users:

- Job seekers applying to competitive roles.
- Career switchers who need to translate experience.
- Professionals who know they are qualified but are underselling themselves.
- Recruiters, coaches, or service providers who need repeatable tailoring.

Primary pains:

- "My resume does not match the role even though I can do the work."
- "I do not know what the ATS or recruiter is missing."
- "I keep rewriting from scratch."
- "I need to apply faster without sounding generic."

Differentiation:

- Shows fit gaps.
- Produces role-specific language.
- Turns job descriptions into document strategy.
- Treats the application as a positioning problem, not just a formatting task.
